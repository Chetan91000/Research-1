## 2. Scientific Integrity Guardrails

To preserve academic integrity and prevent fabrication:
1. **No Simulated Results:** Under no circumstances should fake benchmark numbers or percentages be inserted into the paper prior to executing physical experiments.
2. **Clear Labeling of Proposed Elements:** All descriptions of the architecture, implementation, and test protocol must be framed accurately as **proposed methodology** rather than finished historical work.
3. **Traceability:** Every claimed finding or theoretical basis must be tied directly to a verified entry in the master bibliography.
